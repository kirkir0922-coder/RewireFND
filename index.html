Functional Neurological Disorder Awareness

Rewire the narrative around FND.

Functional Neurological Disorder is real, complex, and often misunderstood. Through education, empathy, and lived experiences, we can build awareness and change outcomes together.

What is FND?

Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) is a condition where the nervous system has difficulty sending and receiving signals properly, leading to symptoms such as movement issues, seizures, weakness, and sensory changes. FND is common, treatable, and deserving of informed, compassionate care.
