What is FND?

Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) is a condition where the brain and body struggle to send and receive signals effectively, which can lead to symptoms such as seizures, movement difficulties, weakness, and sensory changes. FND is common and treatable, and people living with FND deserve informed, compassionate care.

About Rewire

Rewire is a modern awareness campaign focused on education, storytelling, and action. By sharing trusted information and lived experiences, Rewire helps communities better understand FND and support those affected.
